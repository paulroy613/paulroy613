How the model works

  • The shim stack is solved as one circular plate clamped at the clamp diameter and free at its outer edge. Its stiffness at each radius is the sum of every shim that reaches that radius, so tapers and crossovers change the shape of the curve.
  • Pressure acts on the port windows, between the port inner diameter and the seat, scaled by the window share of the circle.
  • Oil flows through the bleed and, in parallel, through the ports and the curtain opened by shim lift (ports and curtain in series). Each uses the orifice equation.
  • Shims are assumed to bend together without separating or sliding. Large-deflection stiffening, gas pressure, cavitation, viscosity and check-valve springs are not modelled.
  • Use it to compare stacks against a baseline. Absolute forces depend on the discharge coefficients, which you can calibrate against a dyno run.
